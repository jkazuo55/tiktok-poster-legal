Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how VideoAutoPost ("the Service") collects, uses and protects information when creators, social media managers, agencies and teams use the Service to schedule, publish and measure videos on TikTok accounts they have connected.

2. Information We Collect

When a user connects a TikTok account through the official Login Kit (OAuth 2.0), the Service collects and processes the following:

  • Authentication tokens: short-lived OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, encrypted at rest.
  • Profile information (user.info.profile): public profile basics needed to identify each connected account.
  • Account stats (user.info.stats): follower count, total likes and video count, used to populate dashboard metrics.
  • Video list (video.list): metadata of videos already published on the connected account.
  • Uploaded video files: content provided by the user for publishing through the Content Posting API (Direct Post).
  • Service account data: email address, workspace and team membership, and minimal usage logs to operate the Service.

3. How We Use Information

The Service uses the information above only to:

  • Authenticate users and the TikTok accounts they have explicitly authorized.
  • Schedule and publish videos to those accounts via the Content Posting API.
  • Display performance metrics in the dashboard.
  • Operate, secure, and improve the Service.

The Service only acts on TikTok accounts that the user has explicitly authorized, and only within the scopes granted on the consent screen.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell user data. Information is shared only with:

  • TikTok, when calling the official TikTok APIs on behalf of the authorized user.
  • Infrastructure providers (e.g. hosting, encrypted database, secure file storage) acting as data processors under contract.
  • Authorities, when legally required.

5. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens and uploaded videos are stored encrypted at rest. Access to production data is limited to authorized personnel and is logged. Tokens are rotated and refreshed using TikTok's standard flows.

6. Third-Party Services

The Service communicates with the official TikTok APIs to perform its functions. Use of TikTok's APIs is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Developer Terms.

7. Data Retention

Authentication tokens are retained for as long as the connection is active. Uploaded video files are deleted after a successful publish or shortly after the scheduled job completes. Metrics history is retained for the period stated in the active plan. Users can disconnect an account at any time, which deletes the associated tokens.

8. User Rights

Users can disconnect TikTok accounts directly from the dashboard, or revoke access from TikTok → Settings → Manage app permissions. Users can also request export or deletion of their Service account data by contacting us.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13, or under the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your rights, please contact us at hello@videoautopost.app.
